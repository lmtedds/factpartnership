24 July 2026 Partnership launch

FACT Partnership launches with SSHRC support

The Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council has awarded a Partnership Development Grant to the Fair and Accountable Canadian Taxation (FACT) Partnership — a three-year collaboration building shared research infrastructure for evaluating tax fairness in Canada.

FACT brings together researchers at the University of Calgary, Carleton University, and the Université de Sherbrooke with two national organizations: Social Capital Partners, a philanthropic policy intermediary, and the Canadian Tax Observatory, an independent non-profit and part of a global network of tax observatories.

The partnership responds to a gap in Canadian public finance. Questions about who bears the tax burden, what public spending actually returns, and who is shut out by the way benefits are administered are usually studied separately, if at all. FACT treats them as one system, across three research themes: tax mix and progressivity; redistribution and public value; and administrative equity and access. An equity, diversity, inclusion, and accessibility lens runs across all three.

The grant provides $199,923 over three years, from March 2026 to March 2029, with additional cash and in-kind support from Social Capital Partners, the Canadian Tax Observatory, and Carleton University. Research design remains academic-led and independent; mobilization is co-developed with the partner organizations so that findings reach policymakers, journalists, and the public in both official languages.

Work is already underway. Two articles are forthcoming in the Canadian Tax Journal, with plain-language companion pieces to follow. Recruitment of graduate students and postdoctoral researchers across the three institutions begins this year.
